It's a facts-based eligibility assessment. You upload the tender document and TenderLogix reads it against your company profile — checking mandatory registrations, compliance documents, B-BBEE requirements, evaluation criteria, and more. You get a GO, NO-GO, or CONDITIONAL GO recommendation with the specific reasons, risk flags, and a compliance checklist. It takes about 60 seconds.
A Bid Pack is a complete submission package generated from the tender document and your company profile. It includes a priced Bill of Quantities (BOQ) with line item rates, all required MBD forms (MBD 1, 4, 8, 9, 15) pre-filled with your company data, a submission checklist, and a Form C1 for references. Print it, sign it, submit it.
Pricing is based on the AECOM Africa Cost Guide 2025/26 — the industry standard used by quantity surveyors across South Africa — adjusted using Stats SA CPAP escalation indices. Where the tender specifies gazetted rates, those are used exactly. Every line item has a confidence rating (HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW). Always verify LOW-confidence rates with local suppliers before submission. The BOQ is a professional starting point, not a final quote.
Your profile is the source of truth for every analysis. The more complete it is, the more accurate your Go/No-Go result and the better your Bid Pack pre-fills. A profile with no directors, no compliance documents, and no turnover data will produce an analysis full of unknowns. You need 60% profile completion to unlock your free trials. It takes about 5 minutes to complete.
The Vault is an optional R99/month subscription that lets you securely store your compliance certificates — B-BBEE, CIDB, CSD, Tax Clearance, COIDA, and more. Vault documents get expiry tracking and instant retrieval. Without the Vault, you can still self-declare your documents in your profile and they carry the same weight in the analysis.
