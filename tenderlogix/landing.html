The Problem

Three reasons you keep losing

Disqualified before evaluation

Your bid never gets to price evaluation. An expired COIDA letter, a missing reference, or a PLI below the threshold — and you're out. The client never even saw your price.

02
You didn't qualify to begin with

The tender required 3 years of healthcare experience and a CIDB Grade 3. You have neither. You spent two weeks preparing a bid you were never going to win.

03
You priced yourself out

You didn't know what competitors charge. You didn't know the NMWA minimum. You guessed — and either lost on price or won a contract you can't deliver profitably.

The tender closed last Friday. You found it on Monday. It was perfect for your business. There are hundreds more you'll never know about unless someone tells you.
