Pricing is anchored to the AECOM Africa Cost Guide 2025/26 — the industry standard used by quantity surveyors across South Africa — and adjusted using Stats SA CPAP escalation indices. Where a tender specifies gazetted rates, those are used exactly. Every line item carries a confidence rating: HIGH (from official tables), MEDIUM (interpolated), or LOW (estimated — verify with local suppliers). The BOQ is a professional starting point, not a final quote. Always verify LOW-confidence rates before submission.
