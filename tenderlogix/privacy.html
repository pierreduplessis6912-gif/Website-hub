Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

We collect only what we need to provide the service. We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your tender documents or company data to train AI models.

1. Who We Are and Who This Applies To

TenderLogix is operated by Website Hub (Pty) Ltd, registered in the Republic of South Africa (Registration No. 2026/024548/07). We are registered with the Information Regulator of South Africa as a responsible party in terms of the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the TenderLogix platform, including company representatives who register accounts, upload documents, and interact with the service.

2. Information Officer

In terms of POPIA, Website Hub (Pty) Ltd has appointed an Information Officer who is responsible for ensuring compliance with this Policy and the Act. Requests to exercise your POPIA rights, data access requests, and privacy complaints may be directed to the Information Officer at privacy@tenderlogix.co.za.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint directly with the Information Regulator of South Africa at www.justice.gov.za/inforeg.

4. Why We Process Your Information (Lawful Basis)

  • Contract performance: To provide the products and services you have purchased — analysing your documents, generating bid preparation materials, storing your compliance certificates.
  • Legitimate interest: To operate and improve the platform, prevent fraud and abuse, and maintain platform security.
  • Compliance with legal obligations: To comply with tax, financial, and regulatory requirements applicable to our business.
  • Consent: To send you marketing communications (you may withdraw consent at any time by replying STOP to any WhatsApp message).

5. How We Use AI to Process Your Information

TenderLogix uses an encrypted third-party AI analysis service to analyse tender documents and generate outputs. When you submit a tender for analysis, the document content and relevant portions of your company profile are transmitted to this service for processing. All transmission is encrypted in transit.

We have taken the following steps to protect your information in this context:

  • Your documents are not used to train AI models. The terms governing our AI analysis service prohibit using API outputs for model training without explicit consent.
  • Document content is transmitted only when you actively initiate an analysis. We do not batch-process your documents in the background.
  • Analysis outputs are stored in your account and are accessible only to you.
  • Director personal details (ID numbers, residential addresses) are not transmitted to the AI unless you explicitly include them in a form pre-fill request.

AI-generated outputs are indicative only. Refer to our Terms of Service for the full disclaimer regarding AI analysis.

7. Document Storage and Retention

Uploaded tender documents are stored in encrypted cloud storage (Cloudflare R2) for the duration of your account. Documents are associated with specific tender analysis runs and are retained to allow you to re-run analyses or download reports.

Compliance certificates stored in the Document Vault are retained for as long as your Vault subscription is active. On cancellation of your Vault subscription, certificates remain accessible until the end of the paid period, after which they are deleted.

On account deletion, all your documents, certificates, and analysis data are permanently deleted within 30 days.

Authentication logs (OTP requests, session records) are retained for 90 days for security purposes and then deleted.

8. Security

We implement the following security measures to protect your information:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher
  • Compliance certificates are stored in Cloudflare R2 with access controlled by session verification — documents are not accessible via direct URL without a valid authenticated session
  • Account access is protected by WhatsApp OTP authentication — no passwords are stored
  • Session tokens expire after 30 days of inactivity
  • OTP codes expire after 5 minutes and are single-use only
  • Rate limiting applies to OTP requests (maximum 3 per hour per phone number)

No security system is completely impenetrable. In the event of a data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as required by POPIA.

9. Your Rights Under POPIA

As a data subject, you have the following rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act:

  • Right of access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Right to correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information
  • Right to deletion: Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention obligations
  • Right to object: Object to the processing of your personal information where we rely on legitimate interest as the lawful basis
  • Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time
  • Right to complain: Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Information Officer at privacy@tenderlogix.co.za. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Cookies and Local Storage

TenderLogix uses browser localStorage (not cookies) to store your session token and account reference on your device. This is necessary for the platform to function — without it you would need to authenticate on every page visit. No tracking, advertising, or analytics data is stored locally or transmitted to third parties for these purposes.

11. Children

TenderLogix is a business platform intended for use by adults operating registered South African businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 years of age. If you believe a minor has submitted information through our platform, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via WhatsApp or email at least 14 days before they take effect. The updated Policy will be published at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
