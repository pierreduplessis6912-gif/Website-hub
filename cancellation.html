Cancellation Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Short version: Website Hub is a monthly subscription. You can cancel anytime from your manage panel. Your site stays live until the end of your current billing period.

1. Subscription nature

Website Hub operates on a monthly recurring subscription model. When you go live, you authorise Website Hub (Pty) Ltd to debit your card each month until you cancel. There are no annual contracts and no cancellation fees.

2. How to cancel

Log in to your manage panel at preview.websitehub.co.za, tap the Account tab, and tap Cancel subscription. You will receive a WhatsApp confirmation within minutes.

You can also request cancellation by WhatsApp to our support number. We will process it within 24 hours.

3. What happens after cancellation

Your website remains live until the end of your current billing period. No further debits will be made after cancellation. Once your billing period ends, your site will be taken offline and your data retained for 30 days before deletion.

4. Build fee

The once-off build fee (where applicable) is non-refundable once your site has been built and delivered. This covers the cost of building your website.

5. Refunds

Monthly subscription fees are non-refundable for the current billing period. If you believe a payment was made in error, contact us within 7 days and we will investigate.

6. Reactivation

If your site has been suspended due to non-payment or cancellation, you can reactivate it at any time. Your content will be restored from our backups within 24 hours of reactivation.

7. Contact

For cancellation queries: billing@websitehub.co.za
