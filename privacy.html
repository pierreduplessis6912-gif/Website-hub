Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026 · Applies to websitehub.co.za and all subdomains

Website Hub is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

1. Who We Are

Website Hub is operated by Pierre du Plessis, based in South Africa. We provide automated website-as-a-service to South African small businesses. For POPIA purposes, Pierre du Plessis is the designated Information Officer.

Contact: privacy@websitehub.co.za

2. What Personal Information We Collect

When you sign up for or use Website Hub, we collect:

  • Identity information: your name and business name
  • Contact information: phone number (WhatsApp), email address
  • Location information: business area, suburb or city
  • Business information: industry, Google Business Profile data (where publicly available)
  • Payment information: billing records and invoice history (card details are processed by PayFast and never stored by us)
  • Usage information: site visit counts, referral activity, subscription status

3. Why We Collect It

We collect and use your personal information to:

  • Build and host your business website
  • Send you updates about your site via WhatsApp
  • Process subscription payments and issue invoices
  • Send service-related notifications (site live, payment due, renewal reminders)
  • Administer the referral programme
  • Improve our service and troubleshoot issues

We will not use your personal information for any purpose other than those listed above without your prior consent.

4. Who We Share Your Information With

We share your information only with the third-party service providers necessary to operate Website Hub:

  • Cloudflare — website hosting, edge computing, and analytics
  • PayFast — payment processing
  • WhatsApp Business API — WhatsApp message delivery
  • DNSimple — domain registration (Standard and Premium plans)
  • AI content generation tools — automated website content creation

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

5. How Long We Keep Your Information

  • Active clients: retained for the duration of your subscription
  • Cancelled clients: records retained for 12 months after cancellation, then deleted
  • Payment records: retained for 5 years as required by South African tax law
  • Prospecting contacts: retained for 60 days from last contact attempt; deleted immediately on opt-out request

6. Your Rights Under POPIA

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information held by us
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Object to the processing of your information
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@websitehub.co.za. We will respond within 10 business days.

7. Opt-Out of Marketing Messages

If you receive a WhatsApp message from Website Hub and do not wish to receive further messages, reply STOP at any time. We will immediately remove you from all future communications.

8. Analytics

We use server-side analytics to measure website traffic and performance. No cookies, tracking pixels, or client-side analytics scripts are used on Website Hub or on the websites we build for clients. No personal browsing data is collected.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Payment processing is handled exclusively by PayFast, which is PCI DSS compliant.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify active clients of material changes via WhatsApp. Continued use of Website Hub after notification constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
