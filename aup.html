Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: June 2026 · Applies to all sites hosted on Website Hub infrastructure

1. Purpose

This policy governs the acceptable use of websites built and hosted by Website Hub. By using our service, you agree that your website will comply with these guidelines at all times.

2. Permitted Use

Website Hub is designed for legitimate South African businesses to establish an online presence. Permitted uses include:

  • Promoting your legal business, products, or services
  • Providing contact information and directions to your business
  • Displaying your portfolio, menu, price list, or service offerings
  • Collecting customer enquiries via contact forms or WhatsApp

3. Prohibited Content

Your Website Hub site may not be used to publish, promote, or facilitate:

  • Illegal activity of any kind under South African or international law
  • Fraudulent, deceptive, or misleading business claims or offers
  • Hate speech, discrimination, or harassment based on race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any other protected characteristic
  • Adult or sexually explicit content
  • Unlicensed financial services, investment schemes, or pyramid structures
  • Counterfeit goods or intellectual property infringement
  • Malware, phishing, or any harmful code
  • Impersonation of another business, brand, or individual
  • Spam or unsolicited bulk messaging directed at third parties

4. Enforcement

Website Hub monitors sites for compliance with this policy. If we identify a violation, we may:

  • Contact you to request immediate removal of the offending content
  • Suspend your site pending investigation
  • Permanently terminate your subscription without refund in cases of serious or repeated violations

5. Reporting a Violation

If you believe a website hosted on Website Hub infrastructure is in violation of this policy, please report it to abuse@websitehub.co.za.

6. Your Responsibility

You are solely responsible for the content of your website and for ensuring it complies with all applicable South African laws, including but not limited to the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act (ECTA), the Consumer Protection Act (CPA), and POPIA.
