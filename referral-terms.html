Referral Programme Terms

Last updated: June 2026 · Standard and Premium plan clients only

Earn one free month for every business you refer that goes live and stays active for 30 days.

1. Who Can Participate

The referral programme is available to Website Hub clients on the Standard or Premium plan with an active subscription in good standing. Legacy plan clients are not eligible to participate unless they upgrade to Standard or Premium.

2. How It Works

  • Each eligible client receives a unique referral link available in their manage panel.
  • When someone signs up using your referral link and their site goes live, your referral is recorded.
  • After the referred client has been live and active for 30 consecutive days, your referral vests.
  • On vesting, one free month credit (equal to your current monthly retainer) is applied to your next invoice.

3. Qualifying Referrals

A referral qualifies when all of the following conditions are met:

  • The referred business signed up using your unique referral link
  • Their site is live and publicly accessible
  • Their subscription is active and in good standing 30 days after going live
  • They are a new client — existing Website Hub clients do not qualify

4. Credit Value

The credit value equals your current monthly retainer at the time of vesting. For example, a Standard plan client at R699/month receives a R699 credit. Credits are not transferable and have no cash value.

5. Legacy Plan Interaction

Legacy plan clients who upgrade to Standard or Premium become eligible from the date of upgrade. Referrals made before upgrading do not qualify retroactively.

6. Credit Expiry

  • Credits are applied automatically to the next invoice after vesting.
  • If your subscription is cancelled before a credit is applied, the credit is forfeited.
  • Credits cannot be carried forward more than 2 billing cycles.

7. Programme Availability

Website Hub reserves the right to modify, suspend, or close the referral programme at any time with 30 days notice to existing participants. Changes will not affect credits already vested and pending application.

8. Fraud and Abuse

Self-referrals, fake accounts, and any attempt to game the referral system will result in immediate disqualification of credits and may result in account suspension. Website Hub's determination of fraudulent activity is final.

9. Consumer Protection Act

This programme is subject to the Consumer Protection Act, 68 of 2008. Nothing in these terms limits your rights under applicable South African consumer protection legislation.
