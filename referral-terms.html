Referral Programme Terms

Last updated: June 2026

Important: The Website Hub referral programme is exclusively available to clients who signed up under a promotional offer (promo code LAUNCH2026 or any other active promotional code). Standard Hub and Hub Pro clients are not eligible.

1. Eligibility

The referral programme is only available to Website Hub clients who subscribed during a promotional period using a valid promotional code. Clients on standard Hub or Hub Pro plans who did not sign up under a promotion are not eligible to participate.

2. How it works

Eligible clients receive a unique referral link in their manage panel. When a friend taps the link and goes live on any Website Hub plan, that counts as one successful referral. Both the referred friend and the referring client must be active paying subscribers for the referral to count.

3. The reward

Upon reaching 10 successful referrals, the eligible client's account will be automatically upgraded to Hub Pro. This includes registration of a .co.za domain in the client's business name. No cash or credit is awarded — the reward is the Hub Pro upgrade only.

4. Promotional period

The referral programme runs for the duration of the promotional period under which the client subscribed. Website Hub reserves the right to close, modify, or extend the promotional period at any time with 30 days' notice to eligible clients.

⚠️ The referral programme may be discontinued at any time. Referral counts at the time of discontinuation will be honoured for clients who have already reached 10 referrals. Partial referral counts will not be compensated.

5. Referred friends

Friends referred via a promo client's referral link will receive the same promotional pricing as the referring client, subject to the promotional offer being active at the time of sign-up. Website Hub reserves the right to limit the number of clients admitted under any promotional offer.

6. Disqualification

Website Hub reserves the right to disqualify referrals that appear fraudulent, self-referential, or in violation of these terms. Referrals from suspended or cancelled accounts will not be counted.
