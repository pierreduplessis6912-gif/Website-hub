Client Referral Programme

Refer a Friend

Active Website Hub clients may refer other businesses to Website Hub and earn account credits when those referrals convert to paying subscribers.

💡 Every active client receives a unique referral link in their manage panel. Share it — when a friend goes live, you earn credit.

Eligibility

You must be an active, paying Website Hub subscriber to participate. Suspended or cancelled accounts are not eligible.

How It Works

  • Share your unique referral link from your manage panel
  • Your friend taps the link, goes through intake, and subscribes
  • Both of you must be active paying subscribers for the referral to count
  • One referral credit is awarded per unique referred business that goes live

Rewards

  • Every 5 successful referrals = 1 month free hosting credited to your account
  • Credits are applied to your next billing cycle automatically
  • Credits are non-transferable and have no cash value
  • Credits expire 12 months after being awarded if unused

Referral Rules

  • Self-referrals are not permitted
  • Referrals from the same physical address or payment method will be disqualified
  • Website Hub reserves the right to disqualify fraudulent referrals
  • Referral counts are visible in your manage panel in real time
Partner Programme

Become a Website Hub Partner

The Website Hub Partner Programme is designed for professionals who work with small businesses — accountants, business consultants, CIPC agents, insurance brokers, chamber members, and others who regularly advise SMEs.

💰 Partners earn R100 cash for every business that subscribes through their unique referral link. No limit on earnings.

Eligibility

Any individual or business operating in South Africa may apply to become a Website Hub Partner. Website Hub reserves the right to approve or decline partner applications at its discretion.

How to Join

  • Send PARTNER to our WhatsApp: wa.me/27840142017
  • Complete the brief onboarding via WhatsApp (name and business type)
  • Receive your unique partner dashboard link and referral WhatsApp link
  • Start sharing — earn R100 for every business that goes live

Commission Structure

  • R100 per referred business that completes payment and goes live
  • Commission is credited to your partner dashboard balance
  • Commission is earned once per referred business — not recurring
  • No commission is paid for referrals that cancel before going live

Payout

  • Minimum payout threshold: R100
  • Payouts are processed via EFT to your registered South African bank account
  • Request a payout from your partner dashboard at any time
  • Payouts are processed within 7 business days of request
  • Banking details must be saved in your dashboard before requesting payout

Partner Rules

  • Partners may not refer themselves or their own businesses
  • Partners may not make false claims about Website Hub's products or pricing
  • Partners may not use spam, bulk messaging, or automated systems to distribute referral links without Website Hub's written consent
  • Partners must disclose their referral relationship when recommending Website Hub to clients
  • Website Hub reserves the right to suspend or terminate partner accounts for violations

⚠️ Commission fraud — including self-referrals, fake businesses, or coordinated abuse — will result in immediate termination and forfeiture of all pending commissions.

Programme Changes

Website Hub reserves the right to modify commission rates, payout terms, or eligibility criteria at any time with 30 days' notice to active partners. Partners who disagree with material changes may close their account and receive any pending earned commissions.
